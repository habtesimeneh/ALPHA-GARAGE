# ALPHA GARAGE — PHASE 1.13
## OFFICIAL BILINGUAL AUTOMOTIVE & WORKSHOP TRANSLATION GLOSSARY

### 1. Overview & Terminology Standards

This glossary establishes the canonical translation standard between English and Amharic for all current and future ALPHA GARAGE modules. Translations adhere strictly to established Ethiopian automotive trade practices, standard accounting terminology (Federal Ministry of Finance / Ethiopian Tax Authority guidelines), and professional technical Amharic.
